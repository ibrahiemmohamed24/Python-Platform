# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome
constructive feedback, and make the project accessible to people with different
backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, publishing private
information, and intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review
comments, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
details on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed
to protect the community.
